# Sales infrastructure: Gumroad checkout, email delivery, customer list, $1 flash sale

Why Gumroad: it takes the payment, emails the product instantly, keeps every buyer's email with a marketing opt-in, sends broadcasts to those buyers, supports discount codes and $1 prices, and has its own Discover marketplace. Fees: about 10% + 50c per sale; payouts weekly to PayPal or bank (check the current terms in Gumroad's settings).

## The user does once (about 15 minutes)
1. Create a Gumroad account with the **new business email** (not the family email). Set the payout to PayPal or bank. Turn on 2-factor sign-in.
2. In Framer: open Callback › Share › **Copy Remix Link**. Send it to Claude, or paste it into product 1's content yourself.
3. Create the two products below (or let Claude draft them in the browser while you watch, and you click Publish).
4. Send Claude both product URLs. Claude wires them into the product page (`LINKS.direct`, `LINKS.checklist`) and republishes.

## $1 flash sale (once infrastructure is live)
- **Goal:** 10 buyers, a first proof of demand and 10 customers on the list. At about $0.40 net each, it's roughly $4 in pocket, so the list and the momentum are the point.
- **Mechanics:** Gumroad offer code `CALLBACK1` on product 1, amount off $28, **limited to 10 uses**, expires in 48 hours. The listed price stays $29 so the discount is real (no fake "was" price).
- **Channels (each post/email needs the user's yes):**
  1. Broadcast to opted-in checklist subscribers (Gumroad › Emails).
  2. Instagram/TikTok/YouTube Shorts: the "3 fixes" video, caption "First 10 people get Callback for $1 with code CALLBACK1, 48 hours."
  3. Story with a link sticker to the product page.
- **After:** ask each buyer for honest feedback (never a scripted review). With permission, quote real feedback on the page.

## Compliance notes
- CAN-SPAM: only email people who opted in; every email needs the sender's identity, a physical mailing address (a PO box works) and a working unsubscribe. Gumroad adds the unsubscribe; the user must add a mailing address in Gumroad settings. Never the family home address in public: use a PO box or virtual mailbox.
- FTC: posts say "I made this"; no fake reviews or urgency (the 10-use limit is real).
- Sales tax: Gumroad collects and remits as merchant of record in most US states and the EU; confirm in Gumroad's tax settings.
