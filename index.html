FAQ

Before you buy

Do I need a paid Framer plan?

You can remix and edit the template on Framer’s free plan. To publish on your own domain you’ll need a Framer site plan.

Which businesses is it for?

Any local service business that wins work by phone or quote request: cleaning, landscaping, pest control, handymen, detailing, moving and more. Swap the copy, photos and colors.

What can I use it for?

One website per purchase, for yourself or one client. You can’t resell or redistribute the template itself.

Where do form submissions go?

You choose in Framer’s form settings: an email address or a webhook to your CRM.

Is the legal text ready to use?

No. The Privacy and Terms pages are starters to adapt. They are not legal advice, so have them reviewed for your business.

What if something doesn’t work?

If your remix link doesn’t work or the template isn’t as described, ask within 14 days for a fix or a full refund.
