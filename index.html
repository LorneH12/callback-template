Do I need to know design or code?

No. You click text to edit it, drag in photos, and change colours once in the style panel. If you can edit a slide deck, you can edit this.

Will it fit my trade?

It ships as a cleaning company, but every section is written for any business that wins work by phone or quote: contractors, landscapers, detailers, pest control, movers.

Where do quote requests go?

Wherever you set them in Framer’s form settings: your email, or a webhook into your CRM or a spreadsheet.

Can I build sites for clients with it?

Yes, one client site per purchase. Buy again for each new client. You can’t resell the template itself.

Is the legal text ready to use?

No. The Privacy and Terms pages are starters. They aren’t legal advice, so adapt them and have them checked for your business.

Why is it cheaper here?

Buying direct skips the marketplace fee. You get the same template, delivered as a remix link by email.
