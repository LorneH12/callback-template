The Website Money-Leak Checklist

21 checks that show where a local service business website loses calls and quote requests, and what each leak costs. Takes 10 minutes with your phone in hand.

For cleaners, contractors, landscapers, detailers and anyone who wins work by phone.

1. The five-second test

Open your site on your phone, count to five, look away. Could a stranger answer these?

What you do, in plain words, above the fold“House cleaning in Mesa,” not “Excellence in every detail.”
Where you workYour city or suburbs in the headline or right under it.
How to reach you, without scrollingA tap-to-call button and a quote button on the first screen.
Why you, in one lineLicensed, insured, guarantee, years in business, rating.
Real photos of your workStock photos of smiling models read as “not real.”

2. The path to money

Every extra tap between “interested” and “contacted” loses people.

The phone number is a linkTap it on your phone: does it dial? If not, every mobile caller has to copy it by hand.
The call button stays visible as you scrollA sticky header or a bottom bar on phones.
The quote form has 5 fields or fewerName, phone, service, ZIP, message. Each extra field costs completions.
You get the form submissionSend yourself a test right now. Check spam. A broken form is the most expensive leak there is.
The visitor sees what happens next“We’ll call you within 1 hour” after they submit.

3. Trust and prices

People call the business that removes their doubts first.

Prices or “from” prices are shown“From $129” filters tire-kickers and reassures real buyers.
Real reviews with names and neighborhoodsLink to your Google reviews. Never invent them.
A clear guarantee“Not happy? We re-clean for free within 24 hours.”
License and insurance statedWith the license number in the footer where it’s required.
A short FAQDo I need to be home? What do you bring? Can I reschedule?

4. Found, fast and fair

The checks most sites skip, and the ones that keep you out of trouble.

Your page title says service + city“House Cleaning in Phoenix | Your Name.” This is what Google shows.
Google Business Profile links to your siteAnd your site links back to your reviews.
Loads in under 3 seconds on mobile dataTest with Google PageSpeed Insights.
Readable text and big tap targetsBody text 16px or more; buttons at least 44px tall; strong contrast.
Privacy policy if you collect form dataSay what you collect and why. Have it checked for your state.
Works with a screen reader and keyboardLabeled form fields, alt text on photos. It’s also an ADA risk if it doesn’t.
Your score: 18–21 you’re in good shape · 12–17 you’re leaking a few calls a month · under 12 your site is costing you real money.
The math: if one job is worth $150 and fixing your leaks brings in just 2 more calls a month, that’s $300 a month, or $3,600 a year, from a site you already pay for.
