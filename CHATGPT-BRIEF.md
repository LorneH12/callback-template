## The project
Callback is a Framer website template for local service businesses (cleaners, contractors, landscapers, detailers), built to make the phone ring. It has tap-to-call everywhere, a 5-field quote form with loading, success and error states, up-front prices, reviews, service area, an FAQ, a phone menu drawer, AA contrast and Privacy/Terms starter pages. The demo brand is fictional ("Finch & Co.", 555 phone number, .example email, Unsplash photos).

**Goal:** first real revenue, then a repeatable template business. **Next milestone:** a $1 flash sale to 10 buyers once checkout, delivery and the social accounts are live.
